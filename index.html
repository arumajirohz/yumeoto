Help & Guide

Getting Started

YUMEOTO is a minimal ambient sound engine designed for focus, relaxation, and meditation.

How to Use

• Select Presets: Choose from pre-built soundscapes like Focus, Relaxation, Meditation, and more.

• Mix Custom Sounds: Click sound cards to enable/disable individual noise types (White, Brown, Pink, Forest).

• Adjust Volume: Use the sliders on each sound card to control individual volumes.

• Stop All: Click the "Stop All" preset to instantly silence all sounds.

Tips

• Use headphones for the best experience

• Experiment with different combinations to find your perfect mix

• This app works offline - no internet required
